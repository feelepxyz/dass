Cut every piece at one stop setting before you change the stop. A batch that reaches the end of a stock length continues on the next length.

Sheet A-300

Råspont

Material: Råspont, 120 × 23 × 4 500 mm. Quantity: 12 lengths.

Caution · verify the stock first

Make sure that your stock matches the material. These sheets are exact only for this material size. One 2.8 mm kerf is used for every piece.

The first 8 stock lengths release all twenty-three 1 175 blanks. Fourteen side boards come first, then nine door boards, at one stop setting.

Change the stop only once per row. Cut every piece in that row, then move to the next stop. The final 397 row uses the labeled P01 / P02 / P03 remainders.

Do not trim loose cladding

Gaps between boards change the joined width. Fix each board to its unit frame before you mark any final edge.

Use the frame edge to set the circular-saw guide. Cut only after the boards are fixed. Sheet A-400 shows each estimated overhang and cut line.

Sheet A-400

Unit drawings and assembly

Each drawing is an orthographic projection off the model. Solid members are the frame. Tinted fields are cladding. Hatching shows the nominal material left for an on-frame cut.

Use the Frame and Cladding controls to change drawing emphasis. The printed drawing keeps every cut line, trim label, and notch note visible.Each unit is drawn on its own sheet, with its assembly steps and its codes beside it.

Stack A

Roof unit

Assembly

  1. Lay RBH1 and RBH2 around RBS1 and RBS2. Center RBC1. Match the diagonals, then fasten the frame.
  2. Center the metal sheet with 30 at each side and 93.1 at the front and rear.
  3. Fit the moving hinge leaf. After the shell is square, fit the fixed leaf and hang the roof.

Stack B

Door unit

Assembly

  1. Build the DBV and DBH frame. Fit DBD1 and match the diagonals.
  2. Fix DCB1 to DCB9 on the inside face. Align DCB1 with the left frame edge.
  3. Use the right frame edge to set the circular-saw guide. Cut the estimated 10 overhang after the boards are fixed.
  4. Cut the two top reliefs shown. Fit the moving hinge leaves, then hang the door with a 10 gap after the shell is square.

Stack C

Left side

Assembly

  1. Build the frame. Match its diagonals before you fix LSC1 to LSC7.
  2. Fix the square LSC boards from front to rear. Align their lower and front edges.
  3. Mark 1 175 at the front and 1 075 at the rear. Set the circular-saw guide and make one gang cut after the boards are fixed.
  4. Use the rear frame edge to set the circular-saw guide. Cut the estimated 10 overhang, then cut the 45 × 45 bottom-front notch.
  5. Do not pre-cut the roof reliefs. Hang and close the roof, then cut only to the scribe.

Stack H

Seat box

Assembly

  1. Install SBH3, SBH1, SBH2, SBS1, and SBS2 after the shell and floor are fixed.
  2. Fix SFB1 to SFB8 to the seat front. Trim the fixed field from the frame landing.

  4. Cut the estimated 36 overhang. Then cut the 270 × 330 opening.
  5. Make sure that the opening clears both bearers and rails. Seal every fresh cladding cut.
